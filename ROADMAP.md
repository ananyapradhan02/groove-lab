# roadmap

1. practice log with a streak: minutes per day and the top ladder tempo reached, stored on the device.
2. rudiment trainer: singles, doubles, paradiddles, flams, with sticking and accent display over the metronome.
3. practice-room mode for teachers: open a shared groove as an assignment, the student plays along and sees the tempo reached.
4. export a groove as a midi file.
5. per-step accents and ghost notes in the grid, carried in the link.
